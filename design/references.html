8 · Award-tier / modern AI SaaS

Steal: type-scale confidence (display text at hero size with selective bold), surface depth as 4–6% luminance steps on near-black, one accent used as light — a glow, never a fill — and bento modularity for the landing page. What does not transfer: gradient glassmorphism and cinematic photography on operational screens would fight Assay’s no-decoration law — this tier is for the landing page and the incident record, not the queue. Takeaways for Assay

  1. Adopt a hard 8px spacing scale and hairline dividers instead of bordered boxes — bucket 1 gets calm from rhythm, not from emptiness.
  2. If we ship dark mode, build it as 4–6% surface steps on #0d–#16 greys with the accent reserved for the one primary action — never inverted light chrome.
  3. Tables: 13px type, 36px rows, right-aligned mono numerics, state as one leading glyph — our Runs and Fields screens should match Grafana's density with Mixpanel's air.
  4. The queue pattern that works is one-expanded-siblings-collapsed with the count in the header — confirms our Decisions layout; resolve stays inside the card, navigation outside.
  5. Empty states and digests: state what will appear, offer one action, one column, one number — the factual tone in bucket 6/7 is our voice already, keep it.
  6. Award-tier restraint transfer. From the Quarn class, take the type-scale confidence and the 4–6% dark surface steps for our dark theme — and give the one permitted accent the role their green plays: the single glowing object on the page is the decision, nothing else is allowed to glow.
