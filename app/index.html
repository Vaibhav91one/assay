Ground truth is exact: every case is a page we mutated ourselves, so the correct answer is known. Value wrong means a confidently incorrect value was returned and stored — silently, into your database.

The gate eliminates every wrong answer. It is not free: correct answers fall from 82.2% to 68.9%, and the difference becomes an explicit refusal rather than a guess. That trade is the product. If you would rather keep the coverage, δ 0.12 removes three quarters of the wrong answers for 4.4 points — the whole curve is below.

Where the thresholds came from

Swept 110 threshold pairs against the mutation set and picked the operating point on the Erratum principle — a no-match is always preferable to a mismatch. Similo proposed this mechanism and explicitly declined to specify a value; no vendor publishes one. These are derived from measurement, and the whole grid ships in results/sweep.json.
