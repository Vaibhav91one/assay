Child Safety

Product Recalls

Chicco takes seriously the responsibility for designing juvenile products that are intuitive and easy to use. Many of our products help ensure the safety of child occupants and/or complement efforts by caregivers to safely care for children. As with any product design process, there may be times when the wide spread use of a product brings to light design or manufacturing issues which need to be addressed. Chicco is committed to understanding and learning from consumer feedback on our products and we proactively undertake measures to improve based on what we learn. If we discover that a product recall is ever necessary, we cooperate with regulatory agencies in this process and fulfill our obligations to the consumers whose trust we depend upon.
